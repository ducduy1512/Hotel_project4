HOTEL PROJECT - N:N PEAK SEASON UPDATE
======================================

Architecture
- MUACAODIEM stores one shared date interval.
- PHONG_MUACAODIEM is the junction table for the PHONG <-> MUACAODIEM N:N relationship.
- PHONG_MUACAODIEM stores the room-specific additive surcharge rate.
- Customer quote and Booking snapshot lookup use the junction table.
- Invoice/Pay snapshots remain immutable and are not recalculated by the migration.
